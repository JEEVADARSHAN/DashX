# DashX

**DashX** is a highly customizable browser extension designed to boost productivity and keep things fun! With a collection of **widgets**, **games**, and **tools**, it transforms your new tab page into a personalized productivity hub.

## 🚀 Features

- **Custom Widgets**: Choose from a variety of widgets like weather, calendar, to-do lists, clock, and more!
- **Interactive Games**: Relax with built-in, light games to refresh your mind during work.
- **Task Manager**: Keep track of your tasks, set reminders, and stay on top of your to-do list.
- **Quick Access Links**: Quickly access your most-used websites and bookmarks.
- **Clean, Minimalist Design**: A simple, organized layout that doesn’t clutter your workspace.
- **Dark & Light Themes**: Choose between sleek dark mode or a vibrant light theme based on your preference.

## 💡 Why Use DashX?

- **Boost Your Productivity**: Stay focused and organized with handy widgets and quick access to your tools.
- **Stay Entertained**: Take a mental break with fun games right on your new tab page.
- **Personalize Your Browser**: Tailor your NewTab page to fit your needs and style, from themes to widgets.
- **Free and Open Source**: Contribute, modify, or simply enjoy without restrictions.

## 🔧 Installation

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Go to your Chrome Extensions page: `chrome://extensions/`
3. Enable **Developer Mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `DashX-extension` folder.

That's it! Your NewTab extension should now be live.

## 🛠️ Configuration

After installation, open a new tab to see your extension in action.

Customize your widgets, theme, and layout by accessing the **Settings** menu in the extension interface.
