About DashX


DashX

Version: v1.0.0.1

DashX transforms your new tab into a sleek, fully configurable dashboard designed to elevate your workflow. More than just aesthetics, it blends powerful productivity tools with intuitive design to help you stay focused, organized, and inspired throughout your day.


Why DashX?

In the modern workspace, digital clutter, scattered tools, and distractions are constant productivity killers. DashX addresses these challenges by consolidating key features in one unified interface:

  • Instant Access: Quickly reach your favorite sites, notes, and widgets in one click.
  • Clean & Distraction-Free: Minimal, elegant layout with options to hide nonessential elements.
  • Performance First: Designed to stay lightweight and fast, your browser won’t notice it’s there.
  • Local-First Storage: All settings and data are stored locally, no cloud tracking, no data sharing.

Productivity Features

DashX brings together tools that help you get more done in less time:

  • Focus Mode / Distraction Blocker: Mute or hide non-essential widgets, silence distractions, and stay in the zone.
  • Smart Layouts & Widgets: Drag, drop, and resize widgets like weather, notes, to‑do lists, bookmarks, and more.
  • Session Snapshot & Recovery: Save and restore tab sets or workspace states, so you never lose momentum.

User Experience & Customization

Every user is different - DashX respects that. You have full control over:

  • Theming, color schemes, fonts, and transparency settings
  • Which widgets appear (or stay hidden)

Support & Feedback

Your feedback is crucial to making DashX better. Found a bug or have a suggestion? Reach out anytime:

  • GitHub: GitHub Repository
  • Website / Contact: jeevadarshan.github.io

Privacy & Security

Privacy is built in not added later. DashX does not transmit or collect your personal data. Everything from your to-do items to your interface settings — remains stored securely in your browser.
